Our History

More Than Just a Utility Provider.

New Paris Telephone was founded at the turn of the 20th century by local residents who understood that connection is essential for community growth. For 125 years, we have evolved alongside technology, moving from copper lines to coaxial cable, and now to a state-of-the-art Fiber-to-the-Home network.

Today, as NPTech, we serve thousands of residents and businesses across Northern Indiana. Our name has changed to reflect our expanded capabilities, but our mission remains the same: Local people serving local people.
