Our History

125 Years of Innovation

For a century and a quarter, New Paris Telephone has been the heartbeat of communication for our area. But as technology evolved, so did we. Today, NPTech stands as the parent company for a diverse ecosystem of services—ranging from high-speed fiber internet and cable entertainment to enterprise IT solutions and structured wiring.

While our name and capabilities have expanded, our core value hasn't changed: Investing in Local Communities We Call Home. We don't just serve these neighborhoods; we live in them, hire from them, and give back to them.
